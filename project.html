Building Logic Gates on a Breadboard

This project focused on the practical implementation of fundamental digital logic gates using a breadboard. It included building and testing AND, OR, NOR, NAND, and XOR gates to understand how digital logic works at the hardware level.

Each gate was constructed using basic electronic components such as integrated circuits (ICs), resistors, LEDs, and jumper wires. The circuits were tested by applying different input combinations and verifying the outputs according to their truth tables. This hands-on work strengthened my understanding of digital systems, signal flow, and hardware-based problem solving.

Objectives
  • Understand the principles of digital logic gates
  • Gain hands-on experience with breadboard circuit design
  • Verify logic operations by testing real input-output behavior
Logic Gates Implemented
  • AND
  • OR
  • NOR
  • NAND
  • XOR
Tools & Components

Breadboard, Logic Gate ICs, LEDs, Resistors, Jumper Wires

Hour of Code — 5th Grade Event

I organized and led an "Hour of Code" session for 5th grade students to introduce them to basic programming concepts using block-based tools. The session focused on making coding accessible and fun while building students' logical thinking and creativity.

Activities included guided tutorials, short challenges, and group tasks where students created simple animations and interactive stories. The emphasis was on exploration, debugging, and celebrating small wins to keep young learners motivated.

Objectives
  • Introduce core programming concepts (sequence, loops, conditionals)
  • Encourage computational thinking and problem solving
  • Build confidence through hands-on, block-based projects
Activities & Tools

Platforms and tools used: Code.org activities, Scratch, and unplugged exercises. Materials included step-by-step guides, starter projects, and mentor-led troubleshooting.

Outcomes
  • Students completed small projects and demonstrated basic program flow understanding
  • Improved engagement with STEM topics and increased curiosity about coding
  • Some students continued with follow-up challenges after the session
