Building Logic Gates on a Breadboard

This project focused on the practical implementation of fundamental digital logic gates using a breadboard. It included building and testing AND, OR, NOR, NAND, and XOR gates to understand how digital logic works at the hardware level.

Each gate was constructed using basic electronic components such as integrated circuits (ICs), resistors, LEDs, and jumper wires. The circuits were tested by applying different input combinations and verifying the outputs according to their truth tables. This hands-on work strengthened my understanding of digital systems, signal flow, and hardware-based problem solving.

Objectives
  • Understand the principles of digital logic gates
  • Gain hands-on experience with breadboard circuit design
  • Verify logic operations by testing real input-output behavior
Logic Gates Implemented
  • AND
  • OR
  • NOR
  • NAND
  • XOR
Tools & Components

Breadboard, Logic Gate ICs, LEDs, Resistors, Jumper Wires
